# Prospective participant instructions — version 1

No actual human pilot has been conducted in this phase. Use this sheet only for a
separately authorized, voluntary local study. No external account or internet is
needed. A coordinator provides an opaque pseudonym and the exact private session
capability path; do not share it or the coordinator's key.

1. Read the purpose, privacy/retention and withdrawal explanation. Choose consent
   or decline explicitly. Timing is optional and must match your protocol.
2. Work on one task at a time. Read its exact claim, saved automated assessments
   and applicable rule versions. A saved match does not prove full award finances,
   current source state or misconduct.
3. Inspect packet citations **and** separately labeled returned context or
   counterevidence. Citation metadata points to preserved source bytes, hashes,
   representation and record locators. No link is automatically fetched.
4. Distinguish observed native fields from decoded/derived identifier
   interpretations. Missing parent fields do not prove absence. Unknown/partial
   pagination, history and revision do not become complete because you accept a
   packet. Contradictory monetary observations may lack authorized comparison.
5. When ready, begin the structured response. Choose one of ACCEPT_EVIDENCE,
   REJECT_EVIDENCE, NEEDS_MORE_EVIDENCE, NOT_APPLICABLE or ESCALATE_FOR_REVIEW.
   These are evidence-quality decisions, not accusations.
6. Select an interpretation code, a principal uncertainty, exact citation and a
   follow-up. Acknowledge every displayed missing proof obligation. The stored
   rationale is explicitly derived from your selections; it is not invented free
   text or proof of understanding. Requests/escalations need a follow-up.
7. Do not type legal names, email, student IDs, IP addresses, credentials or other
   identifying information. Only menu choices are persisted. Invalid raw input is
   discarded without being copied into logs.
8. `q` saves/stops; resume requires the exact session capability and unchanged
   evidence/task order. An interrupted task is re-presented, and its abandoned
   timing is unmeasured; elapsed time across process epochs is never invented.
9. `w` withdraws without asking for a reason. All earlier responses are excluded
   from aggregate performance statistics, even after completion. Withdrawal is
   terminal. The coordinator handles the agreed private-batch deletion process.

Opt-in timing separates presentation-to-begin-response (reading/exposure interval)
from begin-response-to-save (structured-entry interval). It does not measure
comprehension, correctness or workload by itself. Timing-off sessions retain no
per-event clock measurements. Pauses, invalid clocks and incomplete tasks remain
explicitly unmeasured/invalid, not zero seconds. No personal ranking or performance
profile is produced.

If evidence changes, tasks are missing, a capability fails, or publication is
interrupted, stop and ask the authorized coordinator. Do not repair source files,
repeat an uncertain submission blindly, bypass protections or contact third
parties through this application. A reviewer cannot authorize financial arithmetic,
production screening or a readiness upgrade.
